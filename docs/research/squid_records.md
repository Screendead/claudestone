## Takeaways
- Speed records are at the floor: 0 gt close ("instant", same tick as input) and 2 gt (0.1 s) open. The fights are over seamless time,
  reset, volume (smallest fastest) and niche categories.
- The smallest records lean on heavy entity tricks: NaN-motion minecarts, copied UUIDs, non-ticking entities at chunk borders, mob AI.
- Every restriction combination is its own category.
- Possible first target, unverified that nothing newer exists: plain "Fastest Seamless 3x3", instant close / 0.15 s open (3 gt) since 2023.
  A 0.1 s (2 gt) open with instant close would beat it; the 3x3 glass etho already reaches 0.1 s.
